# Template-fit elements: survey and tests

[한국어](2026-11-18-11-35-17-template-fit.ko.md) · [Skill](../skills/template-fit/SKILL.md)

This record explains where the rules in the `template-fit` skill come from. It is evidence, not an instruction; follow the current skill.

## Survey, 2026-11-16

- Five keywords (stage curtain, awards trophy, laurel wreath, invitation envelope, lighting effect). For each, the first five different templates in MiriCanvas's default search order were opened as 1200 px previews and compared side by side. No sort control was shown, so "popular" means the top of the default order.
- Elements were used as parts, not scenes: curtains tied at the left or right edge and cut off by the canvas, swags across the top, light beams from above, confetti, a small trophy or ribbon, a wreath around the title, corner ornaments.
- Styles were flat vector illustration or photorealistic material (velvet, metallic gold). Palettes were red/burgundy, black with gold, navy, ivory/beige and soft pastel. Formats ranged from 1:1.41 portrait posters to 16:9 and 3:1 bands, with the title in the center.
- A same-day bundle of 20 finished, nearly square curtain scenes, about half in clay, felt, knit or paper textures, needed stretching to fit those templates and mixed styles with them.

## Test 1, 2026-11-16: stage-curtain parts, 20 items

- Made with the rules that became this skill: 6 edge panels, 5 top swags, 2 corner drapes, 2 single tassels or tiebacks, 2 translucent light beams, 1 confetti cluster, 2 bottom bands. 12 photorealistic and 8 flat, no textured crafts, no text.
- Several first tall panels and top bands failed preflight with a short side below 700 px (`cropped size 572x1981`), which set the 2.5× ratio limit.
- Placed on six of the surveyed template previews: four fit, one fit partly (flat corners on a photographic gold template), and one did not fit (a 1.74× swag covered a poster title). That led to the rule to keep hanging folds short.
- All 20 were uploaded and submitted for review that day.

## Test 2, 2026-11-18: laurel parts with plugin 0.10.0, 4 items

- A fresh Codex run read the 0.10.0 `template-fit`, `png-element` and shared skills and followed them. No upload, CSV upload or review submission.
- The native tool returned first sources with long sides of 1374–1862 px. Three of four first attempts came back too elongated (2.90×, 3.02×, 4.62×). `ratio` and `crop` both failed them, and they were regenerated rather than stretched.
- Finals: edge panel 1.20×, top garland 2.10×, open hexagon frame 1.43×, bottom trim 1.47×. 4 of 4 are part shapes, 3 flat and 1 photorealistic, no text, margin (0,0,0,0) and ratio PASS on all four.
- On a 1080×1528 poster mock-up with a title box and a date line (mirrored copy for the panel pair), decorative pixels inside the title and date areas measured 0 px in all four layouts.
